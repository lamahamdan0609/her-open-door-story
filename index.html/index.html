A STORY ABOUT BEGINNING AGAIN

THE OPEN DOOR

She had learned to be quiet.

Not because she had nothing to say, but because home had never felt safe enough to say it.

↓ Scroll to continue

Then one night, she chose herself.

She left with a small bag, her sketchbook, and no idea where she would go.

SOMEWHERE ALONG THE WAY

She met an artist.

He was sitting alone with a canvas, painting the world exactly as he imagined it.

"You draw?"

It was the first question he asked her.

They discovered they loved the same things.

Colour. Stories. Creating. Dreaming.

Their friendship grew quietly, one painting at a time.

TOGETHER

What started as a friendship became a shared dream.

They spent mornings creating and nights imagining what their work could become.

Slowly, the empty walls around them began to fill with colour.

YEARS LATER

They opened their first gallery.

A place where people could come to see their art, their stories, and everything they had created together.

Sometimes leaving isn't the end of your story.

Sometimes it's where you finally begin.
